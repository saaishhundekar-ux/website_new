Who Are We

An Emerging Research-Based Seed Company from the Heart of Karnataka

Utphala Agritech Pvt Ltd is dedicated to providing dependable hybrid vegetable seeds in India. Strategically located in Haveri, Karnataka, our R&D develops high-yielding, disease and stress tolerant hybrids with quality parameters as per market requirement.

  • Hybrids bred from native and exotic/imported germplasm
  • Focus crops: Tomato, Chilli, Bhendi, Cucurbits & Melons
  • Offices in Davanagere, Haveri, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, expanding to Guntur & Vilupuram
