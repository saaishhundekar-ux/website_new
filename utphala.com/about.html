Our Story

Dependable Hybrid Vegetable Seeds, Rooted in Research

Utphala Agritech Pvt Ltd is an emerging research-based seed company dedicated to providing dependable hybrid vegetable seeds in India. Our base is strategically located in Haveri, at the heart of Karnataka in South India, a region with a rich tradition of vegetable and spice cultivation.

Our R&D efforts involve developing innovative hybrids from both native and exotic/imported germplasm, bringing together the resilience of local varieties and the performance of global genetics.

We believe in sustainable agriculture that focuses on long-term profitability for the farmers and responsibility toward the environment.
